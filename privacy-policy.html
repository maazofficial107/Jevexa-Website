Privacy Policy

Your trust is our paramount asset. We design our AI architecture with data isolation, minimal retention, and cryptographic protection by default.

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 · Version 2.1

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how JEVEXA AI Automation Studio ("JEVEXA", "we", "us", or "our"), operating via jevexa.net and associated subdomains, collects, processes, stores, and protects your information when you use our web platform, AI Chatbot, Content Generation workspaces, credit wallet, and related automation services (collectively, the "Services").

By accessing or using JEVEXA, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue using the Services immediately.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. We only collect information strictly required to authenticate your account, maintain your credit balance, and execute requested AI workflows:

  • Account & Authentication Credentials: When you register or authenticate via Supabase Auth (email/password or OAuth providers such as Google or GitHub), we store your primary email address, full name, profile picture URI (if provided by OAuth), unique user UUID, and account timestamp metadata.
  • Credit Balance & Transaction Ledger: To power our credit wallet, we maintain records of your current balance, welcome grants (100 free credits upon verified registration), one-time credit top-ups, usage deductions, and idempotency request logs.
  • AI Prompts & Uploaded Assets: When using the AI Chatbot or Content Generation workspaces, prompts, documents (PDF, TXT), and images are transmitted over encrypted TLS 1.3 to our Cloudflare Worker proxy for transient execution.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP address, device user-agent, operating system, and request headers for rate limiting, DDoS prevention, anti-abuse measures, and automated session maintenance.

3. AI Processing & Third-Party Inference Providers

JEVEXA utilizes an enterprise multi-provider routing architecture combining Google Gemini, Groq Cloud (Llama 3.3 70B & Llama 3.1 8B), and OpenRouter to guarantee 99.9% uptime and zero-delay failover.

  • Transient Inference Only: Your queries are processed transiently to produce your requested output. We do not sell your personal prompts or generated outputs to data brokers.
  • No Model Training on Private Data: Our commercial API agreements with upstream providers (Google Gemini Commercial API, Groq Enterprise API) explicitly prohibit using customer API inputs and outputs to train underlying foundational models.
  • Untrusted Data Encapsulation: All user inputs and attached files are encapsulated as untrusted client data in system boundaries, preventing prompt leakage or unauthorized cross-session data contamination.

4. Data Security & Isolation

We enforce multi-tiered defense-in-depth security across every architectural layer:

  • Row Level Security (RLS): All Supabase PostgreSQL tables enforce strict Row Level Security policies. Users can only query and read their own credit balance, transactions, and session state.
  • Edge Proxy Protection: All traffic terminates at Cloudflare edge workers with automated rate limiting, WAF (Web Application Firewall), DDoS shielding, and modern HTTP/3 and TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Zero Credit Card Storage: Payment checkouts are conducted via PCI-DSS compliant sandbox and payment gateway partners (such as Safepay). JEVEXA servers never touch, collect, or store full credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank credentials.

5. Credit Wallet & Idempotency Safety

Our billing engine employs atomic two-phase reservations and automatic release mechanics:

  • Zero-Lock Guarantees: If an upstream AI provider times out or returns an error, reserved credits are automatically restored or reconciled. Users are never charged for unanswered requests.
  • Fixed Rates & Token Metering: Chat messages are billed at transparent, fixed tier credits (1 Lite / 2 Pro / 4 Max / 8 Ultra), while content generation is metered transparently by token consumption.
  • Non-Expiring Active Credits: Paid credit packages are one-time additions to your account wallet and do not force surprise recurring subscription rebills.

6. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Protections)

Regardless of your geographic jurisdiction, JEVEXA honors the following data subject rights:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You may request an export of your account details, credit ledger, and ticket communications at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update your display name and email via your Account Settings panel.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Upon written request, we will permanently purge your account record, authentication references, and associated usage records from our active database within 30 business days.

7. Contact & Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions, compliance requests, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out directly to our Data Protection team:

  • Email: privacy@jevexa.net or hello@jevexa.net
  • Live Support: Available 24/7 via the JEVEXA Support Button in your studio workspace.
  • Domain: https://jevexa.net
