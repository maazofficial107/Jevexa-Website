The Studio Vision

Automating the Future of Modern Business

JEVEXA bridges cutting-edge foundational AI models with frictionless, production-grade business execution. No fluff, no subscription traps—just high-performance intelligence on demand.

Our Core Mission

Modern businesses, agencies, and entrepreneurs are overwhelmed by fragmented digital labor: drafting copy, summarizing complex documents, replying to customer inquiries, and managing repetitive communications. Traditional software adds complexity, while raw LLM interfaces lack enterprise reliability, privacy controls, and predictable pricing.

JEVEXA was built to fix this. We created an integrated AI Automation Studio that unifies 10 essential business tools into a single, high-speed workspace powered by intelligent multi-provider routing and transparent credit economics.

Multi-Model Failover

We believe downtime is unacceptable. Our backend cascades across Groq Llama 3.3 70B (ultra-low latency), Google Gemini (multimodal reasoning), and OpenRouter with automatic zero-delay failover.

Fair Credit Economics

Never get locked into forced monthly subscriptions. JEVEXA utilizes a one-time credit wallet: 100 free welcome credits to start, fixed per-message rates on AI chat, and 20% savings on annual packages.

Privacy & Data Isolation

Your business data is strictly yours. System queries are ephemeral and transient. We enforce Supabase Row Level Security and never permit third-party foundational models to train on user inputs.

Engineering & Global Infrastructure

JEVEXA is engineered as an ultra-low-latency edge application:

  • Edge Compute: Hosted globally across Cloudflare Workers in over 300 cities worldwide, delivering sub-50ms TTFB (Time to First Byte).
  • Database & Auth: Powered by Supabase PostgreSQL with strict cryptographic token verification and ACID-compliant transaction safety.
  • Zero-Lock Credit Engine: Atomic two-phase credit reservations guarantee your wallet is never deducted or held if an upstream API fails or times out.
