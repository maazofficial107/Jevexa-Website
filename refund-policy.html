Refund Policy

Transparent, fair refund rules for one-time JEVEXA credit packages. Try every tool risk-free with 100 welcome credits before purchasing.

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 · Version 1.0

1. 7-Day Refund Eligibility

JEVEXA AI Automation Studio ("JEVEXA") offers a clear 7-day refund guarantee on all paid credit packages (Lite, Pro, Max, and Ultra) under the following conditions:

  • Time Window: Your refund request is submitted within 7 calendar days of the original purchase timestamp.
  • Usage Threshold (< 10% Consumed): Less than 10% of the credits granted by that purchased package have been consumed at the time of the request.
  • Account Standing: The account has not violated our Acceptable Use Policy or engaged in automated abuse.

2. Non-Refundable Cases

  • Heavy Usage (≥ 10% Consumed): Once 10% or more of a credit package has been used across AI models, web search grounding, or document processing, the package is ineligible for a refund due to direct upstream AI compute costs already incurred.
  • Expired Window: Requests received more than 7 calendar days after purchase.
  • Free Welcome Credits: Promotional signup credits (100 free credits) carry no cash value and are non-refundable.

3. Failed Requests & Automatic Credit Reconciliation

If an AI provider call fails, times out, or returns an upstream error, our atomic two-phase reservation system automatically releases the held credits back to your wallet. If you ever believe credits were deducted in error due to a platform incident, contact us and we will verify your ledger and restore affected credits promptly.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request an eligible refund, please email us with your account email address and order/receipt reference:

  • Email: hello@jevexa.net or billing@jevexa.net
  • Support SLA: Email Support: Response within 24 hours (Mon–Sat).
  • Processing Time: Approved refunds are issued back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.
